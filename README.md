One-line office joke: "I put 'per my last email' in my will, so my family knows I tried." (65 chars). Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint" bounty, $2 in USDC.

`main.ts` scores 10 candidate lines 0–2 on the judge brief's criteria (genuine one-line office joke, funny surprise payoff, one line, fresh specific detail, clear and tight), picks the winner at runtime, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, then re-reads and validates the gate (1–200 chars, one line, non-blank) and fails loudly otherwise.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
